# Customer Letter — RecoverIQ

## Dear Seller,

Every day, sellers can be charged fees or miss reimbursement opportunities even when operational evidence exists that could support a recovery claim.

The difficult part is not simply finding a charge.

The difficult part is answering:

> **Can this charge actually be challenged with the evidence available?**

Today, evidence may exist across receiving, preparation, packing, and returns workflows. A recovery process has to connect a financial charge to the correct unit, find the relevant upstream evidence, interpret what that evidence proves, and avoid making a claim when the evidence is incomplete or contradictory.

That is what we are building with **RecoverIQ — an Evidence-First Recovery Manager**.

RecoverIQ takes a fee or reimbursement event and builds a traceable recovery case:

**Charge → Unit → Upstream Evidence → Evidence Interpretation → Decision → Claim**

For every charge, RecoverIQ produces one of four outcomes:

- **SUPPORTED** — the available evidence supports a recovery claim.
- **CONTRADICTED** — the evidence indicates that the charge should not be claimed.
- **UNCERTAIN** — the evidence is insufficient or genuinely ambiguous and requires review.
- **SILENT** — the available evidence does not establish support for the charge.

The system does not invent missing evidence.

It does not turn uncertainty into a confident claim.

It does not redo the work of the Receiving, Prep, Pack, or Returns Managers.

Instead, it uses their evidence as the foundation for a defensible recovery decision.

### What the seller receives

For every recommended claim, RecoverIQ provides:

1. The original charge.
2. The matched unit.
3. The upstream evidence used.
4. The interpretation of that evidence.
5. The claim decision.
6. The recoverable amount.
7. The evidence supporting the decision.
8. A clear explanation of what cannot be claimed and why.

This creates a **Recovery Case File** rather than a black-box prediction.

### Why this matters

A recovery system should not be rewarded for recommending the largest number of claims.

It should be rewarded for recommending claims that can actually be defended.

Our guiding principle is:

> **Don't maximize claims. Maximize defensible claims.**

RecoverIQ is therefore designed around evidence precision, traceability, and conservative handling of uncertainty.

If the evidence is strong, we make the claim clear.

If the evidence contradicts the claim, we stop it.

If evidence is missing or ambiguous, we send it for review rather than guessing.

That is how we turn operational evidence into recovery decisions that another person can inspect, understand, and trust.
