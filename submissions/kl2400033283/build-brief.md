\# RecoverIQ — Build Brief



\## Project



\*\*RecoverIQ — Evidence-First Recovery Manager\*\*



\## Track



\*\*RCY Recovery Manager\*\*



\## Problem



Recovery decisions should be based on defensible upstream evidence rather than simply identifying charges that appear recoverable.



The Recovery Manager receives charge and reimbursement information and connects each charge to the available unit-level evidence from upstream operational managers.



The system must determine whether the available evidence:



\- supports recovery of the charge,

\- supports the charge and therefore contradicts recovery, or

\- is insufficient to make a defensible decision.



\## Goal



Build a conservative, evidence-first Recovery Manager that maximizes defensible recovery decisions rather than maximizing the number of claims.



The system must preserve traceability from:



```text

Charge

&#x20; ↓

Organization + Unit

&#x20; ↓

Upstream Evidence

&#x20; ↓

Evidence Interpretation

&#x20; ↓

Recovery Decision

&#x20; ↓

Supporting Evidence / Review
